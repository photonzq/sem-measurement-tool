        Mouse and keys
        

          
          
0no tool: drag to pan, wheel to zoom (or click the active tool's button again)

          
1 2 3 4 5tools: contour, ruler, angle (3 clicks), angle between 2 lines (4 clicks), line profile

          
clickcontour: add a vertex to the selected contour, or start a new one when no contour is selected; other tools: place the next point (snapped when Snap is on)

          
Ctrl+clickcontour: insert a vertex into the nearest segment; holding Ctrl shows that segment and where the vertex lands

          
click, Shift+click a vertexcontour: select it in the vertex window; add or remove it

          
Alt+clickplace the point exactly where clicked, without snapping

          
drag a pointmove a vertex or a measurement's point; with Snap on it snaps when released (Alt: not); a profile's end does not snap, but the profile turns square about its other end

          
right clickcontour: delete the vertex under the cursor; other tools: delete the measurement under the cursor, or cancel the one being placed

          
wheel, touchpad pinchzoom about the cursor

          
middle drag, Space+drag; drag with no toolpan

          
touch: two fingerspinch to zoom, move together to pan

          
touch: one fingerno tool: pan; with a tool, a tap clicks, a drag from a point moves it, any other drag pans

          
penas the mouse

          
N Cdone with the selected contour: the next click of the contour tool starts a new one; close or open the selected contour

          
the list under Measurementsclick or tap: select one; its box, or Ctrl+click: add or take out; Shift+click: from the row clicked last; Ctrl+A: all; double click or double tap: a profile's plot, a contour's vertices; arrow keys: the next one

          
Downloada contour: its vertices (CSV) or GDS; a profile: its samples (CSV) or its plot (PNG); a ruler or an angle: its row (CSV); several: one row each, and the vertices or GDS of the contours among them; the same for all

          
H Vturn the image so that the selected ruler is horizontal / vertical

          
S Gsnap on or off; edge map on or off

          
Tshow or hide the vertex window (the selected contour)

          
Pshow or hide the profile window (the selected profile); it opens by itself for a new profile, and on a double click or double tap on a profile under Measurements

          
Esquare the selected profile to the edge it crosses, whatever the angle; new and moved profiles up to 15° off square are squared while the option under Snapping to edges is on (Alt with the click or the release: as it is; Ctrl+Z right after: back as it was)

          
drag the square of a profilewiden or narrow its band (the profile must be selected)

          
click on the profile plotplace marker A, then B, then move the nearer one; snapped to the steepest slope while that option is on (Alt: exactly); drag a marker to move it

          
touch: press and hold a pointits menu: delete, select or insert after (a vertex); delete or rename (any measurement); show the plot or square to edge (a profile)

          
arrow keysmove the selected vertices by one pixel width on screen (Shift: ten)

          
Backspacethe last point being placed; else the selected vertices, the last vertex (contour tool) or the selected measurements

          
Deletedelete the selected vertices, else the selected measurements, contours included

          
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Yundo / redo (edits, measurements, calibration); while a measurement is being placed, undo first takes back its last point

          
F + -fit to window; zoom

          
Ctrl+O / Ctrl+Sopen image / save session

          
Esccancel the measurement being placed, or "Set origin"; close a menu
